Research & Open Source Pipelines

1. JWST "Little Red Dots" in IllustrisTNG (ongoing 2026)

Tracking LRD analogues to z=0 to test whether they become today's massive BCGs, and how AGN feedback quenches them. With A. Kapathia (MPIA, Heidelberg). Click a figure to zoom.

2. Galamo: Automated Morphological Classification

Open-source Python package for galaxy analysis on DECaLS imaging, combining ML and statistics for morphology, kinematics, photometry and spectra. galamo.org

3. AGN Flux Disentanglement via PFVG (active)

Pipeline separating AGN flux from total galaxy flux, using COSMOS variability data. With M. Sudan, A. Sarkar, H. Chand.

4. Galspar & RAGA (active)

  • Galspar: precise photometry and morphology on FITS images.
  • RAGA: automated statistical morphology studies via GALFIT.

5. vdisp_fit (2025)

Stellar velocity dispersion for SDSS galaxies. Fits a MILES template convolved with a Gaussian using robust χ2 minimisation. IUCAA Visiting Student Project with G. C. Dewangan.

6. Dynamics of Open Star Clusters (2025)

N-body simulation with fourth-order Runge-Kutta, tracking trajectories and cluster stability. With S. Singh (IIT Delhi).

7. Crab Pulsar Spin-Down (2024)

Archival X-ray timing 2015-2023 for spin-down rate and braking index, plus timing noise and glitches.

8. Alzheimer MRI Deep Learning (2025)

CNN for staging Alzheimer's disease and segmenting structures such as the hippocampus. With H. Kaur.
